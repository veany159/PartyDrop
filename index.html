Good to know
How far ahead should I order?

48 hours for Drop-Off, 72 hours for Set It Up. Peak dates fill up: book early.

Do you deliver to my villa or Airbnb?

Yes. Tell us the property, unit and how to get in. We coordinate with your host or front desk if needed.

It's a surprise. How does that work?

Mark it as a surprise at checkout and give us a contact who's in on it. We only message that person.

What about the heat?

Everything travels chilled in our box. Keep refrigerated and serve within 3 hours for the best experience.

Can I change my order?

Free changes and full refunds up to 72 hours before delivery.
